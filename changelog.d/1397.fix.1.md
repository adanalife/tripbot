The silent-disconnect watchdog now counts recovery *attempts*, not just the ones that worked. `tripbot_obs_silent_disconnect_restarts_total` gains a `result` attribute (`ok`, `failed`) and is recorded before the error check, so a recovery loop that is running and failing is visible — through the 9h41m outage on 2026-08-05 it attempted a restart every 60s, failed every time, and the counter read zero throughout.
