The silent-disconnect watchdog now counts recovery *attempts*, not just the ones that worked, and labels them per platform. `tripbot_obs_silent_disconnect_restarts_total` gains `result` (`ok`, `failed`) and the `service_platform` label every other OBS metric already carries, and is recorded before the error check — so a recovery loop that is running and failing is visible, and the twitch, tiktok and youtube legs no longer share one series. Through the 9h41m outage on 2026-08-05 the watchdog attempted a restart every 60s, failed every time, and the counter read zero throughout.
