The Postgres driver is `pgx/v5/stdlib` rather than `lib/pq`. `lib/pq` picked up seven advisories — GSS auth completing without mutual proof, `.pgpass` credential disclosure via `hostaddr`, a scram CPU denial of service, and four more — with no fixed release available at any version, so `govulncheck` on `main` could not go green again, and a permanently red check says nothing about the next real vulnerability. `pgx` is actively maintained and was already in the module graph: GORM's Postgres driver wraps the `*sql.DB` opened here rather than dialing its own, so the swap is the driver name and one `text[]` column type.
