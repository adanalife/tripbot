`!audio` now takes an album name as well as a bed or a SomaFM channel, so the album bed can be narrowed to one album on the music share instead of shuffling everything on it together — `!audio ambient` reaches `streambeats-ambient` without typing the prefix, and `!audio album` still shuffles the whole share. Albums are the share's subdirectories, read live, so music dropped on the NAS is selectable without a deploy. The console's `/api/audio` gained the matching `album` field and album list.
