Background audio is now selectable per platform from the console: SomaFM, the car-hum drone, or an album from the mounted music share. The album shuffles and advances to the next track when OBS reports the current one ended, and `/api/audio` reads and switches the live bed. The audio watchdog runs on every platform now (any platform can select any bed) and only applies its SomaFM outage recovery while SomaFM is the selected bed.
