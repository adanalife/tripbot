**Season-2 clips are known to the database before any of them airs.** `videos` gains a `corpus` (`s1`/`s2`/`s2fast`) and a `speed` column; a season-2 piece's name is read in its own shape, so a runtime-created row is dated at the piece's offset into its original rather than at a year in the 2030s; `!jump`/`!goto` and `!daytime` stay inside the corpus on screen; and `!speed` reads driving speed over real time, so a 6x clip reports a sixth of what its frames suggest.
