`!help` lists the commands you can run. It used to answer with one line out of a rotating set of tips, numbered `(3 of 11)` — which told a viewer there were eight more and gave them no way to reach any of them except by running it again. It is now an alias of `!commands`, alongside `!command`, `!controls` and `!hello`. The rotation itself is unchanged and still runs on the Chatter timer, where one line at a time is the whole idea.
