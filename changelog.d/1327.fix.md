Outbound chat no longer reports an offline platform as a failure. The rotating chatter and the timed jobs Say on a schedule regardless of whether anything is live, so on Facebook — where a comment can only be posted to a live video — every offline hour was logging an error per tick and forwarding it to Sentry. The gateway's two state replies (409 nothing live, 503 platform refused the call) now carry sentinels, and `Say` logs those at warn; a genuine upstream failure keeps its error level.
