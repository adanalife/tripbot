The internal API serves event-log insights for the console: `GET /api/insights/commands` (per-command runs, distinct users, refusals, refusal reasons, and the top unknown tokens viewers try), `GET /api/insights/guesses` (!guess volume, accuracy, players, per-state breakdown), and `GET /api/insights/footage` (per-clip plays plus avg/max chatter samples, labeled by the clip's geocoded place). All take `?days=N` clamped to 1..90, exclude bots, and are backed by new kind+date / date indexes on `events`, `viewer_samples`, and `video_plays` (migrations 048/049).
