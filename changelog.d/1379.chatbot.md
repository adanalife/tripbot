`!guess` and timewarps now land in the permanent events log. Every answerable guess writes a `guess_submitted` event pairing the normalized guess with the state actually on screen (right or wrong) and how far off it was in centroid miles, stamped with the clip being guessed at; guesses at footage with no known state are skipped so accuracy rollups stay honest. Every playhead warp — `!timewarp`, a correct guess, a gift effect — writes a `timewarp` event naming who triggered it, the source, and the clips it left and landed on.
