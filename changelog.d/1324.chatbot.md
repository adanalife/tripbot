A background-audio switch now waits 5 seconds before it reaches OBS, so a mis-click on the console — or a fumbled `!audio` — can be corrected before the stream's audio changes. A second switch inside the window replaces the first rather than queueing behind it. `!audio` announces the wait (`Switching to Breaker, StreamBeats by Harris Heller in 5s`), and `/api/audio` ships the waiting switch as `pending` so the console can show it.
