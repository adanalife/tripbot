`pkg/contract` now owns the per-platform `gateway-<platform>` Service names and the gateway HTTP port. They were agreed across tripbot's cdk8s, the platform-gateway repo's cdk8s and infra's Argo without any generated contract holding them, so a rename would have broken consumers silently instead of failing a drift test. tripbot's own cdk8s reads the nine `<PLATFORM>_API_URL` values through the contract instead of hardcoding the FQDNs; the rendered manifests are byte-identical.
