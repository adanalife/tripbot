`!goto` is now an alias of `!find`, since that is what chat keeps reaching for it to do. A `!find` (or `!goto`) whose whole query is a state name — `!goto utah` — jumps straight to that state. Jumping to a state also works as `!state <state>` (`!state ma`); bare `!state` still says where the van is, and `!jump <state>` keeps working.
