`bin/stage-streambeats` — stages bought StreamBeats albums onto the music share the album bed plays from, taking the Bandcamp .zip files as downloaded and naming each directory `streambeats-<genre>-<album>` so the bed can select all of them, one genre, or one album. It carries the album→genre table, which is the part the archives can't tell you: Bandcamp tags every album "Electronic, lofi", including the synthwave ones. Dry run by default, never deletes, and safe to re-run after each batch.
