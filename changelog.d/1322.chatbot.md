`!song` names the track from its own filename tags, so a StreamBeats song announces as `"Holosmith" — Breaker, StreamBeats by Harris Heller` rather than repeating the label and the album twice with a track number on the end. The console's now-playing line drops the track number too. An `!audio` switch now names only what it switched to (`Switched to Breaker, StreamBeats by Harris Heller`) and leaves the song to `!song`.
