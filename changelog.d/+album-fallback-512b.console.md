The album is no longer a selectable bed: the radio on its default station is every platform's default, and the album library (StreamBeats and wooderCZ's albums, a fixed list) is only what OBS boots on and what the audio watchdog rides out a radio outage on. `GET /api/audio` drops `album`, `albums`, `groups`, `shuffle` and `pending.album`, and the `audio.bed` event drops `album`, `shuffle` and `pending.album`; `!audio` no longer takes an album.
