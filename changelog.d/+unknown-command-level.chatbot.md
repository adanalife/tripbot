**An unknown `!command` in chat is no longer logged as an error.** A viewer typing a trigger the bot doesn't have — a typo, or one they know from another channel's bot — was logged at `Error`, which pkg/telemetry turns into a Sentry event, so each distinct token minted its own Sentry issue against the free-tier budget. It now logs at `Info`: still in the console and Loki, still attached to real events as a breadcrumb, at no quota cost.
