`viewer_samples` now records the platform's concurrent-viewer count and whether anything was broadcasting, read from the gateway's new `GET /v1/viewers` (migration 047). The existing `count` column keeps its meaning — the chatter total, who has spoken — because redefining it in place would make the series collected since 2026-07-06 unreadable. A platform that publishes no viewer number writes NULL rather than 0, so a rollup can tell "nobody counted" from "nobody watching".
